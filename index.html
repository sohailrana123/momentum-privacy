MOMENTUM · SWEET MOMENTS STOKE LTD

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

1. Who we are

MOMENTUM is an internal business operations and reporting system developed for SWEET MOMENTS STOKE LTD. For privacy enquiries, contact info@sweetmomentsstoke.co.uk.

2. Information we may process

Depending on integrations enabled, MOMENTUM may process store and order identifiers, order dates and statuses, purchased items, transaction amounts, platform fees, refunds and operational reporting information. Where an authorised platform integration supports customer service, MOMENTUM may temporarily access customer names, contact details, delivery details and message contents as needed to handle an enquiry or respond to an order. This is a planned capability and is not a claim that every integration currently supports customer messaging. MOMENTUM is designed not to maintain a permanent database of customer profiles or message contents; actual handling by connected services, execution histories and logs must be verified.

3. Why we process information

We use information to manage orders, prepare sales reports, reconcile transactions, investigate operational issues, maintain business records and improve business operations. Where supported by authorised platform integrations, MOMENTUM is also intended to enable staff to view and respond to customer enquiries through a central dashboard, including AI-assisted draft replies subject to appropriate review. We do not sell personal information.

4. Legal basis

Where personal data is involved, processing must have an appropriate lawful basis under UK data protection law. Depending on the activity, this may be a contractual necessity, legal obligation or legitimate interests. The applicable basis should be documented for each processing activity.

5. Service providers and integrations

MOMENTUM may receive information from authorised third-party delivery and business platforms, such as Uber Eats, and use service providers to operate integrations and reporting. The current development uses n8n for workflow orchestration. Customer messaging functionality will depend on each platform's API permissions and terms. Where AI services are introduced for response drafting, their processing arrangements must be assessed and disclosed before use. Information is subject to applicable platform terms and access permissions. We do not share personal information with unrelated third parties for their independent marketing.

6. Security

We aim to apply appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, including access controls, authentication and restricted integration credentials. No online system is completely secure.

7. Retention

We intend not to retain customer names, phone numbers, delivery addresses or message contents in a permanent MOMENTUM customer-profile database. Temporary processing may still occur in application memory, third-party services, workflow execution histories, error records, backups and technical logs. These systems and their retention settings must be verified and appropriately configured. Business transaction records may be retained for applicable accounting, operational and legal purposes. Specific retention periods are still being assessed.

8. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and other rights under UK law. To make a request, email info@sweetmomentsstoke.co.uk. You may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

9. Changes

We may update this policy as MOMENTUM's integrations and processing activities change. Updates will appear on this page.
